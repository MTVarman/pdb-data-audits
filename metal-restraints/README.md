# Two default metal restraints in cctbx/PHENIX and their exact-value fingerprints in the Protein Data Bank: zinc at 2.30 Å and chlorophyll magnesium at 1.895 Å

Michael T. Varman · [ORCID 0009-0001-9453-0226](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9453-0226)

The scripts reproduce the default cctbx/PHENIX restraints on zinc and chlorophyll magnesium, and count sites whose distances sit on those exact values. `repro/` prints the restraint builder's output; `data/scan/`, `data/zinc/` and `qm/` are the archive counts and the chlorophyll calculation.

Note (all versions): [10.5281/zenodo.24150338](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24150338).

Scripts and tables are copied byte-for-byte from `Varman_metal_restraint_defaults_v1.0_supplementary.zip` on Zenodo record 24150339, the latest version of the note. The fragment coordinate files stay in the zip.
