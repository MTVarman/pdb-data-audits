# Anchor bonds of heme c and light-sensing pigments that are missing from Protein Data Bank connectivity records, and the 2.0 Å line that decides most of them

Michael T. Varman · [ORCID 0009-0001-9453-0226](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9453-0226)

`scan.py` measures heme c, phycobilin, biliverdin, photoactive yellow protein and retinal anchor contacts and records whether a connectivity record is present. The contact tables are in `data/`.

Note (all versions): [10.5281/zenodo.24153068](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24153068).

The script and tables are copied byte-for-byte from `Varman_antenna_bond_records_v1.0_supplementary.zip` on Zenodo record 24153069, the latest version of the note.
